Hi, I'm Vipul. I started Urban Edge to fix one simple problem.

In 2023 my younger cousin Aman had his first job interview. We spent a whole Sunday going from shop to shop. Either the shirt looked good and cost too much, or it was cheap and the collar was crooked after one wash. He wore a borrowed shirt in the end.

That day I decided to do it differently. Urban Edge is built around plain, well-cut menswear with clean photos, so you can see exactly what you are buying. Our slogan is Redefine your Style, and it starts with one good shirt that fits.

Message me on WhatsApp any time we are open. I read the messages myself.
